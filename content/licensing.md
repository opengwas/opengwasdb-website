## Non-commercial research

Free. Download whole stores or query what you need, and cite OpenGWAS and the original studies.

## Commercial use

A commercial licence gives you access to OpenGWAS for its duration. What you download is yours to use, within each source's own terms. Every analysis's page shows its source's licence.
